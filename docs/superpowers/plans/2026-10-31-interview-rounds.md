**Goal:** Give CareerOS one deterministic answer to "what interviews do I have coming up, how prepared am I, and which rounds have I not debriefed": agent-written round records with a light debrief, a few reserved `interview.*` ledger events, state derived from those events plus validated record fields, read-only `careeros interview` views, and an `interview` skill that records rounds and debriefs through the commands.

**Spec:** `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-31-interview-rounds-design.md` (source of truth). Where this plan and the spec differ, the spec wins; every addition is marked **proposed** in section 8.

## 1. Scope and non-goals

**In scope:** round records (`rounds/<rnd_id>.md`) with an optional debrief block; `interview.*` events; derived round state (`scheduled`, `held`, `debriefed`, `cancelled`, `needs_debrief`, `needs_update`); `careeros interview round add|list|show|held|cancel|reschedule`, `debrief`, and the read-only `upcoming` and `needs-debrief` views with prep status; `INT001`-`INT009` and `INT100` in `careeros validate`; changes to the `interview` and `follow-up` skills; docs; version 0.9.0; an invented-data end-to-end acceptance.

**Non-goals:** the story bank; offer tracking; "waiting on a response" and thank-you reminders; email follow-ups; linking interviewers to outreach person records; a GPT workspace counterpart; learning from ratings; importing existing `interview-prep.md` or `activity.md` history. Rounds never change a job's status. The feature is **not a security boundary** and must never be described as one.

## 4. Global constraints (verbatim from the spec where it has them)

- No new dependencies; Python `>=3.11`; deterministic apart from the documented clock use; every derivation takes `now`; one clock read per operation, after the lock; the ledger clock floor (`clock_skew` when `now` is earlier than the latest event of any type).
- Round record: `rounds/<rnd_id>.md`, YAML frontmatter via the strict loader (no aliases, anchors, merge keys, duplicate keys or unknown keys), body optional and unparsed. Keys: `schema` (1), `id` (`rnd_`, immutable, equals the file stem), `job` (an existing job id, immutable), `stage` (`recruiter_screen`, `hiring_manager`, `technical`, `system_design`, `panel`, `final`, `other`), `format` (`phone`, `video`, `onsite`, `take_home`, `other`), `scheduled_for` (an ISO date `YYYY-MM-DD` or UTC datetime `YYYY-MM-DDTHH:MM:SSZ`), `interviewers` (0 to `MAX_INTERVIEWERS` mappings `{name, title}`, `title` may be empty), optional `debrief`. All strings at most `MAX_TEXT` (500) characters with no control, bidi or invisible characters. Any problem makes the record invalid (`INT001`); an invalid record feeds nothing and is never repaired.
- Debrief block: `rating` (`strong`, `mixed`, `weak`), `questions_asked` (at most 10 strings), `do_differently` (at most 10 strings), `next_step` (string), `next_step_date` (optional ISO date). The rating is the user's own words mapped to the enum; no code or skill invents one.
- Events (reserved prefix `interview.`): `round_added` (`artifacts = [rel, "sha256:<hex>", "job:<job id>", "when:<scheduled_for>"]`), `held` and `cancelled` (`source` `user` or `observed`; `cancelled` needs a reason), `rescheduled` (`artifacts = ["when:<new scheduled_for>"]`, action names the date; the record's `scheduled_for` is rewritten first, in the same operation), `debriefed` (`artifacts = [rel, "sha256:<hex>"]`, audit only), `rejected` (audit only, deduped on the entity's latest event of any type, needs a reason, entity must be an existing `rnd_` id, nothing appended for an unknown entity). Unknown `interview.*` types are errors (`INT005`). **A malformed event (missing or unsupported `source`, a missing reason on `cancelled`, a bad entity id, a missing or invalid `job:`/`when:` element on `round_added`, a missing or invalid `when:` element on `rescheduled`) is reported as `INT005` and is NOT applied to derived state, and a round that has only malformed events gets no flags entry.**
- Round state: flags `added_at`, `held_at`, `cancelled_at`, `debriefed_at`, `cancel_reason`; label first match `cancelled`, `debriefed`, `held`, `scheduled`. Legal transitions (else `wrong_state`): `held` needs `scheduled`; `cancelled` needs `scheduled`; `rescheduled` needs `scheduled` (any number of times); `debriefed` needs `held` and no earlier `debriefed`; each of `held`, `cancelled`, `debriefed` at most once; `cancelled` is terminal. `needs_debrief` = label `held`. `needs_update` = label `scheduled` and the record's `scheduled_for` is in the past: a date-only value is overdue once `now` is at or after the start of the next UTC day; a datetime is overdue once `now` is after it.
- Derived-from table (spec 6.0): round identity, which events happened, `held`, `cancelled`, `debriefed` come from the ledger only; `scheduled_for`, `stage`, `format`, interviewers and debrief contents come from the validated record (editable source data); an orphan record or one whose `id` or `job` disagrees with its event feeds nothing; a hand edit never changes identity or what happened and nothing repairs a record. **Incomplete rounds** (valid `round_added` event, record missing, invalid or mismatched) keep identity, label and history from the ledger with every record field unavailable (`null`, `record` = `missing|invalid|mismatched`): `round list` includes them (`incomplete`), `round show` returns identity and history, `upcoming` and the `needs_update` group exclude them, the `needs_debrief` group includes a held one (flagged), `held`/`cancel` still work, `reschedule`/`debrief` are refused `record_unavailable`; nothing recreates or repairs the record (the user restores it or hand-creates one with the same `id` and `job`).
- Rules constants (versioned rules module): `UPCOMING_DEFAULT_DAYS = 14`, `MAX_INTERVIEWERS = 10`, `MAX_DEBRIEF_ITEMS = 10`, `MAX_TEXT = 500` (the last three bound inputs only).
- Prep status of a round (by its job): `none` if the job has no `interview-prep.md`; `checked` if the file's current text hashes (the same way `careeros check` hashes a draft) to the `sha256` of a `draft.checked` event whose entity is the job and whose action records a pass; otherwise `drafted`. The prep file is read as a regular file, never through a symlink, at most 1 MiB; anything else is `none`. The action text is a constant shared between `careeros check` and this reader, pinned by a test.
- Views: `interview upcoming [--days N]` (default 14): rounds labelled `scheduled`, not `needs_update`, with `scheduled_for` within `N` days of `now` inclusive, soonest first then by id. `interview needs-debrief`: group `needs_debrief` (held rounds, oldest `held_at` first) and group `needs_update` (overdue scheduled rounds, oldest `scheduled_for` first, rounds on a job in a terminal status left out of this group; a held round on a terminal job still appears in `needs_debrief`). Orderings are total and independent of input order. **No view or list is capped or truncated.** Rows carry `incomplete` and `date_unrecorded` where they apply. Views create no file, event or lock file.
- Commands: state-changing commands take the lock, check the chain (`ledger_corrupt`), read the clock once after the lock, refuse `clock_skew`, write the file first and the event second in a transaction and roll back both on any failure. `round add` refuses a job in a terminal status (`wrong_state`) and a live (non-cancelled) round with the same job, stage and date (`duplicate`); `held`, `cancel` and `debrief` are allowed after the job reached a terminal status; `debrief` is refused unless the round is `held`. Refusal codes: `not_found`, `wrong_state`, `duplicate`, `invalid_input`, `record_unavailable`, `clock_skew`, `ledger_corrupt`. Exit codes 0 ok, 1 refused, 2 usage error or no workspace; `--json` prints one object. A refusal on an existing round appends at most one deduped `interview.rejected` and changes no derived state.
- **Interrupted updates (spec 7.1; rollback does not cover a killed process):** file replaced atomically then event appended atomically, so a death between them leaves the new file and no event. `reschedule`: the record keeps the new date, views use it, `INT008` warns and rows/`round show` carry `date_unrecorded: true`; recovery is re-running `round reschedule` (allowed when the record already holds that date). `debrief`: the record holds a block, the round stays `held` and stays in `needs-debrief`, an unrecorded block is never a debrief (`debrief_recorded: false`), `INT009` warns; recovery is re-running `debrief`, which replaces the unrecorded block. Nothing repairs automatically.
- `INT` codes (read-only; a corrupt ledger line never crashes the section; record files read as regular files, never through symlinks, with outreach-equivalent size bounds): `INT001` error record shape; `INT002` warning orphan record; `INT003` warning round event whose record file is missing (incomplete round, message says to restore the file or hand-create a record with the same `id` and `job`); `INT004` error record `id`/`job` disagree with the event or one id under two jobs; `INT005` error illegal sequence / unknown `interview.*` type / malformed event; `INT006` warning the round's `job` names no saved job; `INT007` warning status consistency (a round `held` or `scheduled` while its job's status is `DISCOVERED` through `APPLIED` including `APPROVAL_REQUIRED`, or a round still `scheduled`/`needs_update` after the job reached a terminal status; the message names the `careeros transition` the user may want and nothing is ever run); `INT008` warning a complete round's record `scheduled_for` differs from the date its latest `round_added`/`rescheduled` event recorded; `INT009` warning a complete round's record has a `debrief` block but the round has no `debriefed` event; `INT100` info a round is `needs_update`. A workspace without `rounds/` and without `interview.*` events is unchanged. `careeros validate` stays entirely read-only.
- Surgical changes to existing code; the user's real workspace is never touched; committed acceptance records hold codes and counts only; skills, `prep`, `offer`, the evaluation and compensation skills, the approvals flow, the Career Memory evidence gate and the GPT workspace are unchanged except where section 6 Task D says; the feature is not a security boundary.

## 5. Review focus (inputs most likely to bite, each pinned by a task test)

1. An orphan or mismatched round record (crash window, hand-copied file, id or job edited) must feed no view or state, while still being reported (Tasks A, B, C).
2. A malformed `interview.*` event must be reported but never applied, and must not create a flags entry; a later valid event of the same kind applies normally (Task A).
3. Hostile files: a symlinked, FIFO, oversized or non-UTF-8 `rounds/*.md` or `interview-prep.md` must never hang or crash `upcoming`, `needs-debrief`, `validate` or any command, and a hostile prep file counts as `none` (Tasks B, C).
4. Date boundaries: date-only vs datetime `scheduled_for` at exactly the overdue instant and one second either side; a hand-edited invalid date; `--days` boundary inclusive (Tasks A, C).
5. Prep status exactness: the file edited after its check goes back to `drafted`; a failed check, a check for another job, or a check of different text never counts; the hash is computed exactly as `careeros check` does (newlines, BOM, trailing whitespace) (Task C).
6. A process killed between the file write and the event append in `reschedule` or `debrief` must be detectable (`INT008`/`INT009`), must fail safe (a crashed debrief stays in the debrief queue) and must be recoverable by re-running the command; exception rollback is not the test (Tasks A, B, C).
7. A round whose record is missing, invalid or mismatched keeps its identity, shows as incomplete in `round list|show`, stays out of date-dependent views, is never silently recreated, and a held one never silently leaves the debrief queue; no view or list truncates (Tasks A, B, C).

- `round.py`: `parse(text, rel) -> (Round | None, list[Issue])` (alias ban, duplicate/unknown keys, closed enums, `id` equals the file stem, `INT001` for every shape problem, hostile-input safe, never raises), `to_bytes(round) -> bytes`, `with_debrief(round, debrief)`, `with_when(round, when)`, `parse_when(value) -> datetime | None`.
- `fold.py`: `fold_rounds(events, now) -> RoundsFold(flags, errors)` (filters audit-only events, reports unknown `interview.*` types, illegal sequences and malformed events through a returned error list of `SequenceError`, never applies a malformed event, creates no flags entry for a round with only malformed events), `apply_event(flags, etype, ts) -> RoundFlags | Refusal`, `round_added_fields(rel, sha, job_id, when)` and `rescheduled_fields(when)` (the only writers of those event shapes; a malformed or missing `job:` or `when:` element is reported as `INT005` and not applied), `rejected_fields(...)`. `RoundFlags` gains `recorded_when` (the date of the latest applied `round_added`/`rescheduled`).
- `derive.py`: `needs_update(round, flags, now) -> bool`, `build_upcoming(rounds, flags, jobs, prep, now, days) -> list[UpcomingItem]`, `build_needs_debrief(rounds, flags, jobs, now) -> NeedsDebrief(needs_debrief, needs_update)`, `status_issues(rounds, flags, jobs, now) -> list[(code, round_id, message)]` for `INT007`/`INT100`. Inputs are plain data; output ordering is a documented total order. Incomplete rounds are passed with a `None` record plus a `record` status: `build_upcoming` and the `needs_update` group exclude them, the `needs_debrief` group includes a held one flagged `incomplete`; rows carry `date_unrecorded`. `record_issues(rounds, flags) -> list[(code, round_id, message)]` gives `INT008`/`INT009` for complete rounds only.

**Tests first (`test_interview_core.py`):**
- record parser: the spec example parses; every enum/shape/hostile-input case gives `INT001` (aliases, merge keys, duplicate and unknown keys, huge ints, NaN, 100k-deep nesting, lone surrogates, NUL, wrong types for every field, 5 MB body, CRLF, BOM); `id` not equal to the file stem; control/bidi characters in names; bad dates (`2026-02-30`, wrong forms, datetime without `Z`, offsets); `to_bytes`/`parse` round trip;

- incomplete rounds and interrupted updates, pure: a `None` record is excluded from `upcoming` and the `needs_update` group and a held one is included, flagged, in `needs_debrief` in the same order as a complete one; `record_issues` gives `INT008` exactly when the record date differs from `recorded_when` (date-only vs datetime are different values), `INT009` exactly when a debrief block exists without a `debriefed` event, nothing for incomplete or orphan rounds; `recorded_when` follows the latest applied event and ignores malformed ones; the reference-model simulator also covers `rescheduled`;
- import-isolation subprocess test: importing `careeros.core.interview` does not import `validation`, `state_machine`, the CLI, `approvals.service` or anything from `careeros.core.outreach`.
